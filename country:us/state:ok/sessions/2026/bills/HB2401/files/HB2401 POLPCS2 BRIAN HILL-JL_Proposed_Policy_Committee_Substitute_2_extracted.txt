9 An Act relating to land application of sludge and
biosolid material containing human waste; prohibiting
10 land application of certain materials containing
human waste upon certain date; prohibiting issuance
11 of certain permits; requiring cessation of certain
actions by certain dates; requiring plan submission
12 by certain permittees by certain date; directing rule
promulgation; defining terms; providing for
13 codification; and declaring an emergency.

16 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA:
17 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
18 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 2-6-501.7 of Title 27A, unless
19 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
20 A. To protect the health and safety of the people of this
21 state, land application of sludge and biosolid material containing
22 human fecal matter is hereby prohibited on and after July 1, 2028.
23 On and after the effective date of this act, the Department of
24 Environmental Quality shall not issue any new permit for the land
Req. No. 15970 Page 1

1 appl ication of sludge or biosolid material containing human fecal
2 matter. Renewal of permits for land application shall be left to
3 the discretion of the Department of Environmental Quality.
4 B. 1. Not later than September 1, 2026, all persons or
5 entities permitted to land apply sludge or biosolid material
6 containing human fecal matter shall reduce the amount of such
7 material land applied by at least twenty-five percent (25%).
8 2. Not later than September 1, 2027, all persons or entities
9 permitted to land apply sludge or biosolid material containing human
10 fecal matter shall reduce the amount of such material land applied
11 by at least fifty percent (50%).
12 C. Not later than September 1, 2026, any person or entity
13 permitted to land apply sludge or biosolid material containing human
14 fecal matter shall submit a plan for full cessation of such actions
15 to the Department of Environmental Quality. The plan shall be
16 compiled and submitted in the form and manner prescribed by the
17 Department. The Department shall promulgate rules to implement the
18 provisions of this section.
19 D. For the purposes of this section:
20 1. "Biosolid material" means sewage sludge containing any
21 perfluoroalkyl and polyfluoroalkyl substance; and
22 2. "Sludge" shall mean sludge as defined pursuant to Section 2-
23 10-401 of Title 27A of the Oklahoma Statutes.
