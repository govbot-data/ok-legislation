17 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
18 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 3-119.2 of Title 70, unless
19 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
20 A. This act shall be known and may be cited as the "Thrive
21 Act".
22 B. As used in this section:
23 1. "Community eligibility provision" means the federal
24 provision created pursuant to 42 U.S.C., Section 1759a that allows

1 scho ol districts to choose to receive federal special assistance
2 payments for school meals in exchange for providing free school
3 meals to all students enrolled in all or selected school sites

5 2. "Identified student percentage" means the percentage of a
6 school district's enrollment that is certified eligible for free
7 meals based on a documentation of benefit of receipt or categorical
8 eligibility as provided for in 7 C.F.R., Section 245.6.
9 C. Beginning with the 2027-2028 school year, school districts
10 with an identified student percentage of forty percent (40%) or
11 higher that opt in to the community eligibility provision of the
12 National School Lunch Program and demonstrate financial need shall
13 be eligible to receive state child nutrition funds for:
14 1. One hundred percent (100%) reimbursement per meal served in
15 a school district with an enrollment of two thousand (2,000) or
16 fewer students;
17 2. Ninety percent (90%) reimbursement per meal served in a
18 school district with an enrollment of more than two thousand (2,000)
19 students but fewer than ten thousand (10,000) students; and
20 3. Eighty percent (80%) reimbursement per meal served in a
21 school district with an enrollment of ten thousand (10,000) or more
22 students.
23 D. By July 1, 2028, and by July 1 every three (3) years
24 thereafter, the State Department of Education, in collaboration with
