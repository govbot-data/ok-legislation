1 SECTION 2. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
2 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1-229.50 of Title 63, unless
3 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
4 As used in this act:
5 1. "Alternative nicotine product" means any noncombustible
6 product that contains nicotine and that is intended for human
7 consumption, whether chewed, absorbed, dissolved, ingested, or
8 consumed by other means;
9 2. "Department" means the Department of Revenue;
10 3. "E-liquid" means a solution that:
11 a. contains propylene glycol, vegetable glycerin,
12 nicotine, nicotine salts, flavorings, or both, and
13 b. is intended to be used in an electronic cigarette.
14 E-liquid does not include cannabis, THC, CBD, or hemp as defined
15 under the laws of this state;
16 4. "Manufacturing" means the process by which an alternative
17 nicotine product is fabricated, assembled, packaged or labeled, and
18 is sealed in final packaging intended for consumer use;
19 5. "Minor" means an individual who is less than twenty-one (21)
20 years of age;
21 6. "Packaging" means any receptacle that contains a finished
22 alternative nicotine product;

8 SECTION 3. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
9 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1-229.51 of Title 63, unless
10 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
11 A. Manufacturers, dealers, wholesalers, and retailers shall
12 comply with the following requirements:
13 1. An e-liquid bottle shall use a child-proof cap that has the
14 child-resistant effectiveness set forth in the federal poison
15 prevention packaging standards, 16 C.F.R., Section 1700.15(b)(1);
16 2. An e-liquid bottle or an electronic cigarette that contains
17 e-liquid shall use a tamper-evident package. The tamper-evident
18 package feature shall be designed to remain intact when handled in a
19 reasonable manner during the manufacture, distribution, and retail
20 display of the e-liquid bottle;
21 3. The label on an e-liquid bottle, electronic cigarette, or
22 alternative nicotine product shall meet the nicotine addictiveness
23 warning statement requirements set forth in 21 C.F.R., Section
24 1143.3; and

7 SECTION 4. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
8 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1-229.52 of Title 63, unless
9 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
10 A. A manufacturer of alternative nicotine products for sale in
11 the state shall provide an attestation under the penalty of perjury
12 to the Attorney General on forms provided by the Attorney General's
13 Office of the following:
14 1. The manufacturer has received a marketing granted order for
15 the alternative nicotine product from the United States Food and
16 Drug Administration (FDA) in accordance with 21 U.S.C., Section
17 387(j); or
18 2. The manufacturer has submitted a premarket tobacco
19 application for the alternative nicotine product and has received a
20 submission tracking number (STN) from the FDA in accordance with 21
21 U.S.C., Section 387(j) and the application remains under review with
22 the FDA for more than one hundred eighty (180) days, the FDA has
23 issued a no marketing order for the alternative nicotine product,
24 but the agency or a federal court has issued a stay order or

1 inju nction during the pendency of the manufacturer's appeal of the
2 no marketing order, or the order has been appealed either to the FDA
3 or a challenge to the order filed with a federal court and the

5 B. The consumable material of the alternative nicotine product
6 shall be manufactured, processed, blended, or filled in the United

1 SECTION 5. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
2 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1-229.53 of Title 63, unless
3 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
4 A. The Department shall assess a civil penalty against a
5 manufacturer, dealer, wholesaler, or retailer for a violation of
6 this act in an amount that does not exceed Five Thousand Dollars
7 ($5,000.00). A civil penalty shall be assessed in addition to other
8 penalties allowed under this act.
9 B. The Attorney General is authorized to investigate and
10 enforce violations of Sections 3 and 4 of this act. Upon
11 determining that a manufacturer, dealer, wholesaler, or retailer has
12 violated any provision of Section 3 or 4 of this act, the Attorney
13 General shall bring a civil action in any court of competent
14 jurisdiction to either: seek injunctive relief restraining or
15 enjoining any manufacturer, dealer, wholesaler, or retailer from
16 continuing to engage in activities that violate Section 3 or 4;
17 recover civil penalties of up to Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00)
18 per violation; or obtain appropriate relief to protect the public
19 interest.
20 C. Civil penalties collected under this act shall be deposited
21 in the Alternative Nicotine Products Compliance Fund.
22 SECTION 6. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
23 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 1-229.54 of Title 63, unless
24 there is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
