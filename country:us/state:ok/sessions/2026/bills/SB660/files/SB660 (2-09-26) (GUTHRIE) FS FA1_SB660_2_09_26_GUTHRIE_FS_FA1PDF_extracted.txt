11 An Act relating to state government; creating the
Oklahoma Foreign Agents Registration Act; providing
12 short title; providing legislative intent; defining
terms; prescribing requirements to register as a
13 foreign agent; assigning certain duties to the Ethics
Commission; requiring certain agent to disclose
14 certain information; requiring certain agent retain
certain records; granting certain investigative
15 authority to the Office of the Attorney General;
providing penalties; providing for the promulgation
16 of rules; construing provision; providing for
noncodification; providing for codification; and
17 providing an effective date.

21 BE IT ENACTED BY THE PEOPLE OF THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA:
22 SECTION 1. NEW LAW A new section of law not to be
23 codified in the Oklahoma Statutes reads as follows:
24
Req. No. 3564 Page 1

1 A. This act shall be known and may be cited as the “Oklahoma
2 Foreign Agents Registration Act”.
3 B. The purpose of this act is to ensure transparency and
4 accountability for political activities and advocacy activities
5 designed to influence public policy that are conducted in Oklahoma
6 on behalf of countries of concern.
7 SECTION 2. NEW LAW A new section of law to be codified
8 in the Oklahoma Statutes as Section 13001 of Title 74, unless there
9 is created a duplication in numbering, reads as follows:
10 A. As used in this act:
11 1. “Agent of a foreign principal” shall mean any person who
12 acts at the order, request, or under the control of a foreign
13 principal and who engages in political activities, lobbying,
14 fundraising, or other political advocacy within this state,
15 including but not limited to publicity agents, public relations
16 counsel, political consultants, or information service employees;
17 2. “Country of concern” means any foreign country designated by
18 the United States government as a hostile foreign adversary or a
19 Country of Particular Concern, whether pursuant to 15 C.F.R.,
20 Section 791.4 or any other provision of law;
21 3. “Foreign principal” means:
22 a. a government of a foreign country or a foreign
23 political party,
24
Req. No. 3564 Page 2

1 b. any entity organized under the laws of a foreign
2 country or having its principal place of business in a
3 foreign country, or
4 c. any person domiciled outside the United States who is
5 not a United States citizen; and
6 4. “Political activity” means any activity intended to
7 influence the policies, actions, or decisions of state or local
8 government or to shape public opinion on behalf of a foreign
9 principal.
10 B. No person shall act as an agent of a foreign principal from
11 a country of concern unless he or she has registered as such with
12 the Ethics Commission. Except as provided in subsection D of this
13 section, registration shall be filed within ten (10) days of
14 becoming such agent and shall be updated annually and upon any
15 material change. Information to be provided by the agent during
16 such registration shall include:
17 1. Name and address of the registrant;
18 2. Identity of the foreign principal;
19 3. The nature of the relationship, including contracts or
20 agreements;
21 4. A summary of activities undertaken or planned;
22 5. The amount and source of compensation;
23 6. A statement of ownership and control of the foreign
24 principal, if an entity; and
Req. No. 3564 Page 3

1 7. Disclosure of political contributions made by the registrant
2 during the preceding twelve (12) months.
3 The Ethics Commission shall prescribe registration forms and
4 maintain a public registry.
5 C. The Ethics Commission shall have civil enforcement authority
6 to enforce the provisions of this act in accordance with the
7 penalties prescribed within the Rules of the Ethics Commission.
8 D. Any person acting as an agent of a foreign principal from a
9 country of concern on the effective date of this act shall, within
10 sixty (60) days, register with the Ethics Commission and disclose
11 any activities undertaken on behalf of or compensation received from
12 the foreign principal during the twelve (12) months immediately
13 preceding such registration. Any person who becomes an agent of a
14 foreign principal from a country of concern after the effective date
15 of this act shall be subject to the registration procedures provided
16 for in subsection B of this section.
17 E. 1. Any informational materials distributed in this state by
18 agents on behalf of a foreign principal from a country of concern
19 shall clearly state that such materials are:
20 “Distributed by [Name] on behalf of a foreign principal from a
21 country of concern. Registered with the Ethics Commission”.
22 2. Agents shall retain a copy of such materials for four (4)
23 years after the distribution of such, and make such available for
24 inspection.

1 3. Agents shall regularly report to the Ethics Commission in a
2 similar manner as other campaign finance reporting with a list of
3 all informational materials disseminated, identifying the date,
4 medium, and general subject matter of such.
5 F. Agents shall maintain a record of all activities and
6 payments related to the foreign principal from a country of concern
7 for at least four (4) years and make such records available to the
8 Office of the Attorney General upon request. It shall be unlawful
9 to willfully conceal, destroy, falsify, or obliterate any records
10 required under the provisions of this act.
11 G. 1. The Attorney General shall have exclusive authority to
12 investigate and enforce criminal violations of this act.
13 2. The Ethics Commission shall refer suspected criminal
14 violations to the Office of the Attorney General. Nothing in this
15 act shall be construed to prevent the Attorney General from
16 initiating an investigation of a suspected violation independently.
17 3. The Attorney General may pursue criminal penalties for
18 willful violations of the provisions of this section, punishable
19 upon conviction by a fine not to exceed One Hundred Thousand Dollars
20 ($100,000.00), imprisonment for not more than five (5) years, or
21 both such fine and imprisonment.
22 4. It shall be unlawful for any contract for compensation
23 between a foreign principal and an agent to provide for compensation
24 contingent upon the success of political activities.

1 H. 1. The Ethics Commission shall promulgate rules as
2 necessary to effectuate the provisions of subsections B, C, D, and E
3 of this section. The Ethics Commission shall not be construed to
4 have the criminal enforcement authority provided for in subsections
5 F and G of this section.
6 2. The Attorney General shall promulgate rules necessary to
7 effectuate the provisions of subsections F and G of this section.
8 The Attorney General shall not be construed to have authority to
9 implement the registration, disclosure, and civil enforcement
10 provisions provided for in subsections B, C, D, and E of this
11 section.
12 I. The requirements of this act shall not be construed as
13 applying to:
14 1. Accredited diplomatic or consular officers recognized by the
15 United States Department of State;
16 2. Officials or employees of a foreign government whose duties
17 are public and nonpolitical; or
18 3. Attorneys engaged solely in legal representation in judicial
19 or administrative proceedings.
20 SECTION 3. This act shall become effective November 1, 2026.
